Project Title:
Customer Churn & Retention Analytics + GenAI

Business Problem:
A telecom company is losing customers due to churn.
The company wants to understand which customers are leaving,
why they are leaving, and which customers are at high risk of churn.

Business Objective:
The goal of this project is to analyze customer behavior,
identify the main churn drivers, find high-risk customer segments,
and provide retention recommendations to management.

Why This Project Is Needed:
Customer churn reduces revenue and increases customer acquisition costs.
By identifying churn patterns early, the company can take targeted
retention actions and improve customer loyalty.

Expected Outcome:
1. Identify churn rate.
2. Find major churn drivers.
3. Identify high-risk customer groups.
4. Compare churn across contract types, services, tenure, and charges.
5. Build a Power BI dashboard for management.
6. Build a GenAI assistant that can answer churn-related business questions.
7. Suggest retention actions based on the analysis.


Key Stakeholders:

1. Customer Retention Manager
   - Wants to identify customers who are likely to leave.
   - Needs high-risk customer segments for retention campaigns.

2. Marketing Manager
   - Wants to understand which customer groups respond better to offers.
   - Needs insights for targeted promotions and loyalty programs.

3. Customer Service Manager
   - Wants to identify service-related issues that may cause churn.
   - Needs information about complaints, service usage, and customer behavior.

4. Finance / Revenue Manager
   - Wants to understand the revenue impact of customer churn.
   - Needs to identify valuable customers who are at risk of leaving.

5. Senior Management
   - Wants a high-level view of churn rate, major churn drivers,
     high-risk segments, and recommended retention actions.


Dataset Understanding:

Customer Identifier:
- customerID: Unique ID of each telecom customer.

Customer Profile:
- gender: Customer gender.
- SeniorCitizen: Indicates whether the customer is a senior citizen.
- Partner: Whether the customer has a partner.
- Dependents: Whether the customer has dependents.
- tenure: Number of months the customer has stayed with the company.

Telecom Services:
- PhoneService
- MultipleLines
- InternetService
- OnlineSecurity
- OnlineBackup
- DeviceProtection
- TechSupport
- StreamingTV
- StreamingMovies

These fields describe which telecom services the customer is using.

Contract & Billing:
- Contract: Month-to-month, one-year, or two-year contract.
- PaperlessBilling: Whether paperless billing is enabled.
- PaymentMethod: Customer payment method.

Financial Metrics:
- MonthlyCharges: Monthly amount charged to the customer.
- TotalCharges: Total amount charged during the customer relationship.

Target Variable:
- Churn: Indicates whether the customer left the company.



Project Architecture:

1. Raw Dataset
   - Telco Customer Churn CSV
   - Contains customer profile, services, contract, billing, charges, and churn status.

2. Python / Jupyter Notebook
   - Load the raw CSV.
   - Check missing values and duplicates.
   - Fix data types.
   - Clean TotalCharges.
   - Create business features.
   - Perform churn-focused EDA.
   - Export a cleaned dataset.

3. PostgreSQL
   - Load the cleaned churn dataset into PostgreSQL.
   - Run business-focused SQL queries.
   - Analyze churn by contract, tenure, services, payment method, and charges.

4. Power BI
   - Connect Power BI to PostgreSQL.
   - Build a clean data model.
   - Create DAX measures for churn and retention KPIs.
   - Build management dashboards.

5. GenAI
   - Connect the cleaned churn data / analysis outputs to a GenAI assistant.
   - Allow users to ask churn-related business questions in natural language.
   - Generate retention recommendations and management summaries.

6. Final Business Output
   - Identify major churn drivers.
   - Identify high-risk customer segments.
   - Recommend retention actions.
   - Present insights through Power BI and GenAI.
